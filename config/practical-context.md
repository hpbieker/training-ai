## Route Context

- Default start/end radius: `0.25 km`.
- Weather fallback: Sørkedalen, `60.0189, 10.5834`.
- Sandungen in Nordmarka is gravel; Sandungen in Vestmarka is road.
- Tryvannstårnet-style climbs and Olav Bulls vei are interval/climbing routes,
  not normal VT1 routes.

## Sensors

- Moxy is normally placed consistently on the right vastus lateralis.
- Activity data may include power, heart rate, VE, VT, BR, Moxy SmO2/THb,
  core and skin temperature, and environmental temperature and humidity.
