# VT2-kapasitet ut 2026

This file is LLM/agent-readable personal training context only. Helper scripts
must not import, parse, or read it directly. Before a daily recommendation, the
agent selects the current plan role here and passes that role to helpers through
explicit arguments such as `--intensity-goal`.

- Horizon: 2026-07-25 through 2026-12-31.
- Primary goal: rebuild and then extend durable VT2 work while sustaining a

## Performance Objective

Re-establish the spring ability to perform long, repeatable VT2 work with
controlled physiological cost, then determine whether the same durability can
be moved from 290 W toward 295 W. Do not promise a higher wattage or more than
the planned 15-17-hour volume density. Both must be earned by stable execution
and recovery.

Use VO2Max work to lift or preserve the aerobic ceiling above VT2. The normal
intensity distribution is two VT2 opportunities plus one VO2Max opportunity,
not three large VT2 sessions.

The plan distinguishes:

- load accounting from Xert;
- Zone 2/VT1 volume and durability;
- VT2 structure, continuity, and progression;
- day-level physiological permission from Garmin, Intervals.icu wellness,
  subjective feel, and Xert recovery.

Xert XSS is a dose and recovery input. It does not define whether low-system
load came from Zone 2 or VT2 and does not replace the structural progression.

## Volume Density

- Normal density: equivalent to about 15-17 hours per seven days when averaged
  over 14-21 days. This is a load-density guide, not a repeating week.
- Reduced density: equivalent to about 11-14 hours during travel, illness
  return, poor recovery, or a deliberately lighter absorption period.
- Expansion: exceed the normal density only when current and recent response is
  clearly good. Add 30-60 minutes to one or two easy rides or the next long
  ride; do not raise VT2 dose and volume density aggressively at the same time.
- Do not force extra low-quality riding at the end of an arbitrary measurement
  window merely to hit a number. Protect the next quality opportunity.
- Seven-day totals may be shown as a familiar monitoring metric, but they do
  not reset the plan, impose weekday roles, or create a quota.

## Continuous Session Sequence

Plan quality as a queue embedded in a continuous string of aerobic days:

- `VT2 primary -> VT2 secondary -> VO2Max -> repeat`, with both VT2 queue
  steps using the same VT2 progression ladder.
- Put at least one genuinely aerobic day after each completed intensity
  session. Add a second aerobic day when the preceding dose, next-day response,
  long-ride placement, or readiness warrants it.
- The result is normally three intensity sessions across roughly six to nine
  days, but there is no quota and no reset after day seven.
- The VO2Max role follows the two VT2 opportunities because it develops a
  different limiter and helps make a given VT2 wattage a smaller fraction of
  aerobic capacity.
- If Xert high/peak recovery or the direct readiness signals do not permit
  VO2Max, but low-system recovery and direct signals permit controlled VT2,
  replace the VO2Max session with VT2 consolidation.
- If neither VO2Max nor VT2 is supported, use Zone 2/VT1. Do not force three
  completed intensity sessions merely to satisfy a recent-day count.
- A downgraded quality day does not advance the quality queue. Attempt the same
  role at the next suitable opportunity.

## Available-Time Placement

Use calendar availability to place the continuous sequence:

- Less than about 75 minutes: recovery or easy aerobic riding. Use a compact
  VO2Max session only when that is the next queue role and the window still
  permits a proper warmup, main set, and cooldown.
- About 75-120 minutes: good for VO2Max, shorter VT2, or easy aerobic riding.
- About 120-180 minutes: good for full VT2 plus aerobic support, or a medium
  aerobic ride.
- About 180-240 minutes: good for VT2 plus substantial VT1, or a medium-long
  aerobic ride.
- About 240 minutes or more: prefer the long Zone 2/VT1 role when it fits the
  quality sequence and recovery. A long ride may also follow a shorter quality
  set when that combination has a deliberate role and will not compromise the
  next quality session.

Do not waste a rare long window on recovery merely because a weekday template
would have called it easy. Equally, do not force intensity into a short window
when the next quality role cannot be executed properly.

### Session B / consolidation

The second VT2 opportunity does not have to be artificially shorter when the
previous progression step was clearly stable. It may take the next step.

Use a consolidation dose at roughly 70-90 percent of the current main-set
duration when:

- the preceding progression session was physiologically costly despite stable
  watts;
- the long aerobic ride or rolling volume is already demanding;
- readiness allows VT2 but does not support a full progression dose;
- the next progression attempt needs protection.

## VO2Max Progression State

The VO2Max opportunity is complementary to the VT2 priority. Do not increase
VO2Max power, interval duration, and repetition count simultaneously.

The current priority is a sensor-calibration block, not automatic continuation
of the historical 60/60 ladder. Use identical Moxy placement and Tymewear
setup in all three comparison sessions:

1. `4 x 4 min @ 340 W`, with 4 minutes easy between repetitions.
2. `2 x 8 x 60/60 @ 380 W`, as the clean, repeatable short-interval
   reference. The historical comparison averaged roughly 383 W, but that
   observed average is not the prescribed target.
3. `5 x 3 min @ 350 W`, with 3 minutes easy between repetitions.

Use the same fixed power target for every work interval in a session. Do not
increase target power, interval duration, or repetition count during this
three-session comparison. Use a fixed `120 W` for all recoveries within and
between sets in all three VO2Max calibration sessions.

Tymewear ventilation is a proxy for aerobic system engagement, not a direct
VO2 measurement. Compare accumulated time at high stable VE, BR behavior,
power stability, and next-day response. Use Moxy for progressive SmO2
desaturation, pause reoxygenation, and THb/data-quality context. Compare
absolute Moxy values only with consistent placement.

The first pending VO2Max step is `4 x 4 min @ 340 W`. A good initial result has
controlled mechanics with less than about 3 percent power fade, high but stable
ventilation, progressive SmO2 desaturation rather than an immediate crash, and
adequate reoxygenation between repetitions. The historical working calibration
anchors are VE roughly 145-165 L/min, BR roughly 60-70/min, SmO2 roughly
10-20 percent late in work, and pause reoxygenation roughly 50-55 percent or
higher. Treat these as athlete-specific comparison bands rather than universal
physiological thresholds.

Classify the session before progressing:

- Stable and informative: complete the next format in the calibration block.
- Under-stimulating: retain the format and add only 5 W at its next comparison;
  do not change duration or repetitions simultaneously.
- Excessive or unstable: reduce the target by 5-10 W at its next comparison.
  Do not replace the missed VO2Max work with extra VT2 in the same session.

After all three formats have usable sensor data, select the ongoing VO2Max
family from the format that provides the best combination of sustained high
ventilation, relevant SmO2 desaturation, repeatable mechanics, manageable Xert
high/peak cost, and good next-day response. Only then define the format-specific
progression. The historical `2 x 8 -> 2 x 9 -> 2 x 10 x 60/60` ladder remains
an option if 60/60 wins the comparison; it is no longer the default pointer.

## Third Intensity Opportunity Decision

Use this order for the planned VO2Max day:

1. VO2Max when Garmin direct signals agree, Xert high and peak systems are
   recovered at the planned time, and the previous quality response was
   absorbed.
2. VT2 consolidation when VO2Max is blocked specifically by high/peak-system
   readiness but controlled subthreshold work remains permitted.
3. Zone 2/VT1 when acute signals, low-system recovery, illness, or accumulated
   fatigue make intensity inappropriate.

This is a downgrade hierarchy for one planned role, not permission to turn
every good day into a fourth intensity session.

## Stability And Progression Decision

Classify every VT2 session before choosing the next one.

### Stable

- All prescribed work is completed at the same target power.
- Mechanical execution remains controlled without material power fade.
- HR per watt, VE per watt, and BR per watt remain acceptably controlled within
  and between blocks for the environment and sensor quality.
- SmO2 behavior and reoxygenation do not show a worsening pattern unsupported
  by the rest of the physiology.
- Breathing and perceived exertion remain consistent with controlled VT2 rather
  than a threshold test.
- The athlete returns to normal well enough to preserve the next planned
  quality opportunity.

Action: advance from the dose actually achieved at the next VT2 opportunity if
at least 48 hours and current readiness permit. A different interval structure,
longer duration, or higher wattage is not by itself evidence against stability.
Do not force completion of intervening structural rungs after a stable result.

### Marginal

- Target power is completed, but physiological cost escalates materially;
- or subjective control is worse than intended;
- or next-day recovery is meaningfully impaired.

Action: repeat the same structural step, use a 70-90 percent consolidation
dose, or reduce the anchor by 5 W. Do not count completion alone as proof that
the step is stable.

### Unstable

- Prescribed power cannot be sustained;
- physiology becomes uncontrolled;
- or the session has to be materially shortened for non-logistical reasons.

Action: step back one duration rung or reduce target power by 5 W. Resume
progression only after a stable result.

Environmental heat and poor cooling must be interpreted explicitly. Do not
mistake a heat-limited response for loss of power capacity, but do not ignore
the resulting physiological cost.

## Zone 2 / VT1 Progression

Track aerobic progression separately from VT2.

- Primary measure: 14-21 day volume density, expressed as an equivalent number
  of hours per seven days for familiarity.
- Secondary measures: duration of the longest aerobic ride, late-ride
  HR/BR/VE-per-watt stability, subjective leg condition, and effect on the next
  VT2 session.
- First extend easy or low-VT1 duration. Do not let routine endurance rides
  drift toward upper endurance often enough to compromise VT2.
- Increase the next long ride or a medium ride by 30-60 minutes when the recent
  training string is being absorbed well.
- If VT2 quality deteriorates while rolling volume rises, hold or reduce
  aerobic volume before lowering the established VT2 anchor automatically.

## Daily Recommendation Contract

Before running `recommend_today.py`, the agent must:

1. Read this active plan and the latest relevant VT2 analysis.
2. Determine the current plan role: VT2 progression, VT2 consolidation,
   VO2Max, long aerobic, medium aerobic, easy aerobic, recovery, or
   interruption.
3. Determine recent 14-21 day volume density, available time, the next quality
   role in the continuous queue, and the latest relevant VT2 or VO2Max result.
4. Pass the resolved role explicitly:
   - VT2 progression/consolidation: `--intensity-goal vt2`;
   - VO2Max: `--intensity-goal vo2max`;
   - long/medium/easy aerobic: `--intensity-goal vt1`;
   - recovery/interruption: `--intensity-goal recovery`.
5. Use Garmin, Xert, Intervals.icu wellness, subjective feel, weather, calendar,
   and location to set the physiological ceiling and executable dose.

In the final recommendation:

- `Fysiologisk mulighetsrom` comes from current direct signals and recovery.
- `Treningsmessig behov` must name this plan, the current plan role, current
  progression step, recent same-family result, and rolling-volume position.
- `Endelig anbefaling` combines the plan role with the physiological ceiling
  and logistics.

Do not call a generic `mixed` helper goal a training plan. Do not let Xert's
target XSS silently replace the selected plan role. If physiology downgrades
the planned role, retain both facts: what the plan requested and what is safe
today.

## Interruption Rules

- Current sickness event: no training.
- First healthy day after sickness: rest or very easy return.
- Travel, poor sleep, or heat may reduce the day without changing the long-term
  plan.
- After 4-7 days without VT2, resume at the latest stable step or one duration
  rung below according to the cause and current response.
- After 8-14 days without VT2, resume one to two duration rungs below.
- After more than 14 days without VT2, use the latest comparable session and
  current physiology to recalibrate anchor and duration; do not assume the old
  rung remains current.
- A single poor day changes the day's execution, not the whole plan.
