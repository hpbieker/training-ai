revisited when updating a skill or script. The skills contain the guidance
used in routine analysis and planning.

## Local paper archive

Store original article PDFs under [papers/](papers/). Existing collections
include [muscle oxygenation](papers/moxy/), [respiration](papers/respiration/),
and [wearable temperature sensing](papers/core/).
Keep one copy per publication and use `author-year-short-topic.pdf` for new
files. An official full-text XML or HTML copy is also useful when a PDF is
unavailable. Verify the title and publication before linking the local file.

Reference a paper by DOI, PMID, or a stable publisher or repository link.
Record whether a source is available locally and what was reviewed. A citation
or abstract can remain in a research bibliography while the full text is
sought. Record the tested protocol, relevant finding, applicability, and
limits in the research note. Link a cited article to its existing local copy
when it appears in more than one note.
