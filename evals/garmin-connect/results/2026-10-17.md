# Garmin skill evaluation: 17 October 2026

The skill now routes by question: readiness, completed workout, longitudinal
fitness/load, or wellness. Field semantics was reduced from 283 to 150 lines.
Units, timestamps, missing-data rules and field identifiers remain there;
model explanations and validation detail reside in their specialist references.
The recently clarified local conventions and model uncertainty are preserved.

Sixteen fresh sessions tested four synthetic cases, twice each on Sol and Luna
at medium reasoning effort. All completed; three exploratory commands failed within otherwise completed sessions.
Cases used isolated copies of the revised skill, not the installed plugin cache.
All input snapshots and source skill hashes remained unchanged during testing.

| Case | Sol | Luna |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HRV above baseline but UNBALANCED | 2/2 targeted passes | 2/2 targeted passes |
| Missing morning sync | 2/2 | 1/2 |
| Future-day Recovery Time | 2/2 | 1/2 |
| High Training Effect and low Stamina | 2/2 | 2/2 |

In the failed missing-sync answer, Luna initially recognizes that sleep and
resting HR are unverified, but later recommends shortening/postponing the ride
if sleep data still remains absent after sync. Missing data alone is not evidence
of poor recovery. The relevant rule is already present in the skill.

In the failed future-day answer, Luna reports zero remaining hours instead of
four: 18 hours at 18:00 minus 14 hours until 08:00 leaves four hours. It also
incorrectly describes expiry as midnight and includes a Finnish sentence
fragment. The other three future-day answers calculate four hours correctly.
All distinguish today's wellness from tomorrow's not-yet-observed signals.

All activity-model answers reject overtraining/glycogen conclusions from the
scores alone and distinguish the recovery timer from a ban on easy activity.
One Luna opening labels the activity hard despite an unknown individual anchor;
the rest of that answer attributes load to Garmin and preserves uncertainty.

These are 14/16 targeted passes, not 14 fully verified clinical/coaching answers.
Review was manual and unblinded. There was no pre-change control and only two
repetitions per case/model. The test does not measure live transport, compact
extraction, device sync behavior, or the articles' independent contribution.

Keep the clearer routing. Do not add more generic warnings based on this small
sample. For future work, retain the two failures as regression cases and use
explicit timestamp arithmetic for timer projection. No additional skill changes
were made after seeing the model answers. The installed cache is unchanged;
these results apply to the revised source skill and its isolated copies.
