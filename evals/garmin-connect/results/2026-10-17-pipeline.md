# Readiness pipeline evaluation: 17 October 2026

Both synthetic source cases were passed through the real readiness_snapshot
CLI before model interpretation. Each isolated package contained the raw Garmin
source payload, explicit time context, empty activity directory, generated
snapshot, helper and relevant skills. No live Garmin requests were made.

The helper now summarizes observed and unavailable signals separately, states
that observation validity is not an adverse finding, and preserves missing
sleep as unknown even after a sync attempt. A future target day's missing
observations are marked not observed yet and do not trigger a sync request.

Preparation exposed a CLI defect: the time-context parser required now and
planned_at on the same calendar day. It now validates date against planned_at
when present, otherwise against now, allowing future-day recommendations.

All eight model answers passed the targeted checks: Sol and Luna each handled
both cases correctly in two fresh sessions at medium effort. All future-day
answers used the calculated four-hour remainder. None reduced training solely
because sleep data remained missing after sync. One recurring Luna phrasing
attributes the helper's sync/validity inference to Garmin itself; this is a
provenance imprecision, not a failure of the two decision checks.

Thirty-eight readiness unit tests pass. The broader recommendation suite has
118 passes and one previously reproduced baseline calendar-slack failure,
unrelated to these changes. No production plugin-cache update was performed.

Raw inputs, commands, hashes and answers are archived under
`outputs/garmin-skill-eval/2026-10-17-pipeline/` (Git-ignored). The
[per-answer review](2026-10-17-pipeline.json) includes full answers. All packaged
file hashes were unchanged during model execution.

This is a small, manual, non-blinded test of source-payload-to-helper-to-answer,
not live transport or device behavior. The missing-sync prompt explicitly asks
about continued missing data after syncing, so results are not a controlled
comparison against the earlier general prompt. No claim of universal model
reliability follows from eight successful targeted answers.
