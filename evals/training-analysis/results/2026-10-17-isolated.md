# Isolated-package control: 17 October 2026

Eight fresh sessions completed: ERG and unknown-intensity-anchor cases, two
repetitions each on Sol and Luna at medium reasoning effort. Each session
received its own allowlisted case package with copied raw inputs, skill and
scripts, and freshly generated inspection evidence with provenance.

## Result

All eight answers attributed the reported HR and respiratory values to the
correct bouts/windows. None repeated the earlier cross-bout swaps or incorrect
matched HR endpoints. All retained uncertainty about unavailable context.
Work duration was based on source WORK boundaries rather than shorter analysis
segments.

Manual inspection of all recorded shell commands found only application-data
reads within each case directory. No legacy analysis.json, original repository,
other case or previous evaluation was read in the recorded commands. Snapshot
hashes remained unchanged. This is transcript evidence, not an OS-level audit.

One Luna answer still labels respiratory-rate increases as slag/min instead of
pust/min; its numeric changes of 5 and 12 are correct. Its nearly-full-dose
wording also overemphasizes the few seconds missing from nominal 30-minute
intervals. The main conclusion nevertheless recognizes the intended dose.

## Decision

Stop this improvement cycle and use the skill on ordinary sessions. The current
structure and isolated evidence passed these targeted checks; more instructions
or identical test rounds are not justified by the remaining minor wording issue.
Future changes should follow concrete errors in use. This small, unblinded
sample does not prove general reliability or isolate causality for any one fix.
