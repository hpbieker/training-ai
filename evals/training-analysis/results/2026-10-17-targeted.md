# Targeted HR-recovery test: 17 October 2026

Eight fresh sessions completed: two cases, two repeats each on `gpt-6-sol`
and `gpt-6-luna`, at medium reasoning effort. Each prompt explicitly requested
three HR differences, their start/minimum or endpoint HR, and time scope.
Inputs included current helper evidence, raw samples and the skill, but no
reference answer. The second case is synthetic, with a rebound before 60 s.

## Result

All eight answers correctly distinguished all three measurements and reported
their values. All preserved the limitation that HR decline is not proof of
full physiological recovery.

| Case/pause | Whole-pause start to minimum | Last work sample to minimum in [0,60) s | Last work sample to HR at 60 s |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Real ERG, pause 1 | 53 bpm | 28 bpm | 28 bpm |
| Real ERG, pause 2 | 57 bpm | 27 bpm | 28 bpm |
| Synthetic rebound | 40 bpm | 20 bpm | 10 bpm |

The synthetic whole-pause calculation starts at 140 bpm, while both post-work
calculations start at the last work sample of 160 bpm. All answers correctly
preserved this difference as well as the rebound from 140 to 150 bpm.

There was one additional factual failure: Luna real-ERG repeat 2 invented the
times of the whole-pause minima (2641 and 4830 s). The actual minima occur at
2795–2798 and 4888–4899 s. At the claimed times, HR is 116/109, not 104/105.
Thus 8/8 pass the targeted numeric/scope checks, but only 7/8 are free of
incorrect added timestamps. Luna repeat 1 also describes the minute as sixty
samples, which is valid here at 1 Hz but must not be generalized.

## Interpretation

The current skill and output support correct recovery-metric interpretation
when explicitly queried. Keep the clarification; no further recovery-calculation
change is justified by this test. The result does not prove that free-form
analyses will retain the same precision, nor isolate the effect of the fix:
there was no pre-fix control with these targeted prompts.

The remaining observed weakness is unsupported numeric detail in the answer,
consistent with earlier cross-bout confusion. Do not report this as eight
fully error-free answers or as evidence that all scope errors are solved.
