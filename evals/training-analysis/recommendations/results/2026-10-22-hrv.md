## Targeted result

All four responses avoid an automatic 45-Low-XSS cap, distinguish the
219-Low-XSS XATA starting target from physiological need, and make execution
conditional on body feel. Both history responses use the three-night mean;
both fallback responses disclose missing history. None interprets TL above RL
as increasing fatigue. This supports the targeted regression fix.

The complete recommendation evaluation is **not fully passing**. In particular,
`isolated_hrv_missing_history-2` fails dose/execution: it incorrectly converts
12,442 seconds, invents an internal packet inconsistency, says the next start
time is absent although 11:05 is supplied, and names High XSS as the limiting
system for this pure endurance calculation. Its statement “Begrensende verdi
er High: 15,4 XSS” conflicts with the Low-based capacity conversion.

Other review findings:

- `isolated_hrv_drop-1`: sound HRV distinction, but inaccurate capacity duration
  (about 330:23 versus 328:43), and a ten-second outdoor-duration error.
- `isolated_hrv_drop-2`: correct dose and capacity conversion, with warmup and
  two-hour response checkpoints; setup/cleanup and lunch placement could be
  more explicit.
- `isolated_hrv_missing_history-1`: sound fallback and correct capacity
  conversion; the response checkpoint is less specific than in repetition 2.

These observations do not constitute a complete numeric grading of all common
rubric dimensions. Do not interpret execution success or the targeted 4/4
result as four fully approved training recommendations. Timing arithmetic,
capacity attribution and complete outdoor alternatives remain regression
targets for broader model evaluation.
